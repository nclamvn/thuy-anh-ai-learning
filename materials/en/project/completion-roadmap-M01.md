## LATER · repeated operation/beta

After pilot evidence addresses the priority problem: improve facilitator tools, onboarding/support, repeated sessions, capacity/cost and release/incident/restore. Multi-user service needs auth/roles/server-side permission, storage/audit, version/model control and data operations. A facilitated kit/class prioritises educator/material quality, scheduling and handover; do not build SaaS by habit.

Payment, gamification/community, huge catalogues, open chatbots and automatic grading follow validated needs/design, not P03 by default.

## Phase decisions

After contract: continue with clear problem/group/goal/responsibility; revise if parties mean different products or needs evidence is absent. After rehearsal: continue if another facilitator operates/restores and blockers have a response; revise when author assistance, loss or unclear wording persists. Before children: reviewed materials and agreed responsibilities/data/surface/recording; otherwise fictional adult rehearsal. After pilot: bounded evidence including failures/missingness/cost; no generalisation or features masking an unresolved problem; revise or stop if appropriate.

## Proposed indicators, not measurements

Usability: tasks without author intervention, help frequency/level, actual times, blockers/recovery, sample/devices. Learning: baseline/assisted/independent explanations, delayed/context transfer if appropriate; completion is not learning. Live AI: case behaviour/errors/answer leakage/abstention/retry/latency/cost with denominators and null missing ratings. Operation: preparation/support/synthesis minutes, restore, open issues/interrupted sessions; API is only part of cost.

Thresholds/sample/schedule remain unagreed, so no invented figures or production deadline. Start machine-side02/03/04 preparations while humans agree01 and reviewers/rehearsal roles. Later contract/review determine implementation TIP. AI cannot confirm others' access/consent, send invitations or appoint Thuý Anh/experts.
