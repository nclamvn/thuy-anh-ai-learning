## Fictional example

DRY-02: the role-player reads A but not B and says “I think 09:00”. The facilitator asks “Is there another card you have not read?” The role-player opens B and revises the time. Observation: found an update after one prompt. Possible interpretations: card layout is unclear, or the reader did not understand the need to read both. Do not label this “lacks critical thinking”.
