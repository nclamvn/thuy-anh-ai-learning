## Before real children participate

A responsible person must confirm age/prerequisites, reviewed content and rubric, informed parental agreement under suitable conditions, voluntary learner participation and a way to stop, response to distress/out-of-scope questions, what data are recorded/who can access/retention/deletion/sharing, and suitable space/equipment. Until these are established, use adult rehearsals with fictional data.

Interest, opening a survey or ticking a software checkbox is not assumed consent. Recording audio, photographs or identifiers requires a separate agreement within scope. Give AI only a permitted copy with unnecessary information removed.

## Proposed first conversation

45-minute draft agenda: 10 minutes on the original motivation and needs evidence; 10 on learners/decision-makers; 10 on format/resources; 10 on one activity/age group/observations; 5 to record agreements, unknowns and owners. No meeting is booked by this document.

Use [interview](../templates/interview-guide.md), [review](../templates/curriculum-review.md), [session plan](../templates/session-plan.md) and [decision record](../templates/decision-record.md). Never fill a real-person record with fictional answers. DEMO examples teach template use only.
