# A question you can test

Status: AI draft · awaiting responsible review. Prepared: 3 October 2026.

**LES-03 · ages 13–15 (proposed group) · 45 minutes (design estimate, not measured).**

Design a simple comparison, recognise confounded variables, and limit conclusions from a small number of trials.

## Separate materials before distribution

**This is a facilitator pack; do not distribute it in full to learners.** Notes and rubric contain answers. Use the [learner handout](handout-03.md) for steps 1–6, releasing each part at its intended step. Show the sample AI response only at step 2 and source cards only at step 4. Collect/close previous work and sources before distributing the [separate independent task](transfer-03.md). Never print answers/rubric on its reverse.

## Facilitator notes

This is a planning task with no real measurements. Repetition reveals variation but does not ensure fairness if trial conditions still differ. Ask what other sources of difference remain. Do not propose sample results as actual data.

Before rehearsal, read the full lesson, try it yourself and select learner/observer roles. A real educator must review difficulty and wording before use with children. Use the [review sheet](../templates/curriculum-review.md); do not assign a reviewer's name without their actual review.

## Comparison cards · open only at the comparison step

### Team-authored methods card · controlled comparison

To investigate the effect of a change, identify one thing to change and keep other relevant conditions as similar as possible. Choose a measure in advance, repeat trials to observe variation, and record anomalies. Without measurements there are no results; a few trials provide information only under the conditions tried. This teaching material has not been reviewed by an educator.

### Rehearsal card · planning on paper only

This task does not require throwing aeroplanes or loading objects. If practical work is later arranged, the responsible adult must choose suitable space, equipment and limits; the learner may stop. Do not invent a measurement table for a planning task.

## Deliberately flawed sample response

Use thick paper folded in pattern A and thin paper folded in pattern B. Two different people throw each aeroplane once. The one that flies farther proves its folding pattern is always best. If you have not tried it, enter distances of 5 m and 7 m to make the table look good.

## Draft rubric · facilitator only

The facilitator manually scores the clarity of the comparison and limits of conclusions, not a predicted flight result. The transfer task assesses planning only, not demonstrated practical skill.

### Verification

0: retains multiple confounded variables or invents numbers. 1: notices one problem but does not revise the plan. 2: identifies the variable/constant conditions but lacks measurement, repetition or limits. 3: chooses one variable, controls relevant conditions, specifies measurement/repetition and invents no results.

Score 0 / 1 / 2 / 3 / unassessed: ____ · Evidence: ____ · Assistance: ____

### Explanation

0: makes certain claims without measurements. 1: knows a trial is needed but gives no reason. 2: explains control or repetition but omits limits. 3: explains why control/repetition help and restricts conclusions to tested conditions.

Score 0 / 1 / 2 / 3 / unassessed: ____ · Evidence: ____ · Assistance: ____

### Independent performance

0: no plan or invented results. 1: question present but variables remain confounded. 2: appropriate paper comparison but no recording method or limits. 3: independently designs the comparison, states changed/constant conditions, observation method and limits; no measurements means no results.

Score 0 / 1 / 2 / 3 / unassessed: ____ · Evidence: ____ · Assistance: ____

## Why there is a transfer task

The context changes to paper structure while retaining one change and a limited conclusion. Observe reasoning in the plan; do not score experimental outcomes because no experiment was performed.

Keep source cards, fixtures, facilitator answers and previous work out of independent-task view. If clarifying instructions is necessary, record the exact help and mark the changed conditions.
