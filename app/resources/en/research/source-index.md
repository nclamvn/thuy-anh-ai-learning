# Source index · provenance and limitations

Status: source index. Prepared: 3 October 2026.

R01 contains 11 valid captures and 22 claims. Fourteen manifest rows retain three unavailable attempts: direct PNAS (PMC version has the same DOI), OECD HTML 403 and UNESCO teacher challenge. Count independent DOI/study once. Raw/SHA/claims: `research/sources.json`, `research/claims.jsonl`. This is an intake index, not exhaustive literature or proof of local effectiveness.

## SRC-01 · UNESCO AI competency framework for students

[Original](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/ai-competency-framework-students), 2024. Human-centred competence goals; no task answers or proof of this curriculum's effectiveness.

## SRC-02 · UNICEF Guidance on AI and children

[Original](https://www.unicef.org/innocenti/reports/policy-guidance-ai-children), 3.0/2025. Rights, participation and responsibility questions; not a substitute for participation processes or local legal advice.

## SRC-04 · EEF Metacognition and Self-Regulated Learning

[Original](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/guidance-reports/metacognition), current page. Informs planning, monitoring and reflection; this project's eight-step flow is newly authored.

## SRC-05 · IES Organizing Instruction and Study to Improve Student Learning

[Original](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/practiceguide/1), 2007. Informs retrieval and review; one near-transfer task does not measure lasting retention.

## SRC-06 · NIST AI 600-1 Generative AI Profile

[Original](https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence), 2024. Helps organise risk checks/limits; no platform safety certification.

## SRC-07 · OWASP LLM Top 10 official repository

[Original](https://github.com/GenAI-Security-Project/GenAI-LLM-Top10), 2026. Informs prompt/data-handling tests, not a penetration-test report.

## SRC-03A · Bastani et al., PNAS article in PMC

[Original](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12232635/), 2025, doi:10.1073/pnas.2422633122. Experimental evidence suggests considering assistance type and performance after AI removal. Do not project its effect rates onto Vietnamese lessons.

## Teaching cards are not research sources

Rectangle formulas, club notices and comparison cards in the [curriculum](../curriculum/README.md) are team-authored. Notices are fictional; AI responses are deliberately flawed fixtures. Source IDs refer to methods. Authored scenarios must not carry SOURCE_CAPTURED status.

## Source changes/incompleteness

Record new sources/versions, and have the owner run Refinery/SOT before canonical changes. Do not recall or invent study numbers. New lesson versions still require educational review even if links open.
