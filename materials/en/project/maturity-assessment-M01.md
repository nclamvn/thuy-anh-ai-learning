## Conditions for greater maturity

**Confirmed rehearsal:** another facilitator runs one lesson through report/restore; record difficulties, all assistance and actual times; fix blocking issues. No child-effectiveness claim.

**Responsible pilot:** educator reviews goal/version; accountable person agrees participation/stopping/data; suitable surface/storage; observation protocol and continue/revise/stop criteria before collection. Simulations cannot satisfy these conditions.

**Repeatable beta:** target operators run multiple sessions, restore data, handle support, and record usability/cost with bounded learning claims. Effectiveness sample/comparator design is separate from rehearsal headcount.

**Production:** after delivery model choice, validate corresponding operations/safety/access/recovery/releases/support. Networked multi-user service differs from one-device demo. No cloud/provider/stack decision in M01.
