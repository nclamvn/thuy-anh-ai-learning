## Our proposed design decisions

Start adult rehearsal with LES-02: an earlier notice and a later update require attention to timing, source authority and uncertainty. An everyday situation makes evidence seeking observable without arithmetic fluency dominating the task. This is the AI team's design rationale, not a family-validated need.

Keep three evidence layers separate: initial work before assistance; revisions after sources or feedback; a new independent task without AI or answer keys. Consider a delayed task in another session if the educational reviewer finds it appropriate. Do not infer progress or rank children from an automatic aggregate score. Language changes may change reading difficulty; do not pool VI/EN effectiveness comparisons before checking task equivalence.

Record session ID, lesson edition, task language, actual assistance, observed duration and observer role. Distinguish behaviour, participant accounts and interpretation. Synthetic rehearsal records do not enter real participant outcomes. Manual scores require work evidence and an accountable reviewer, not merely a UI selection.

If reviewers disagree, retain both judgements and the disagreement, discuss rubric interpretation and revise the draft. Do not fill gaps with AI inference. Worked calibration examples belong to the facilitator area and are not validation data.

## Work needed before conclusions

- An educational reviewer examines VI/EN tasks, difficulty, rubric and delayed assessments; records a version-specific decision.
- Another adult operates independently and records friction without step-by-step AI coaching. Rehearsal establishes operation under those conditions, not educational effectiveness.
- Thuý Anh/Lâm confirm users, needs, data responsibility and participation conditions. The survey introduction cannot replace respondent data not supplied here.
- Obtain the World Bank full text and Kestin supplementary information; examine allocation, attrition, analysed sample, comparator, secondary outcomes and conflicts before using them in pilot decisions.
- Before a live AI trial, freeze tasks, model/version/prompt, budget and request authorization; retain original responses and human review decisions. Do not put child data in the preparation benchmark.

Track these in the workbench and P03 records. Names or evidence typed into a checklist do not authenticate identity, establish participation agreement or approve use with children.
