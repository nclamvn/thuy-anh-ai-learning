# Source discovery and selection · R01

3 October 2026. AI-proposed protocol requiring owner maintenance. Targeted scan across 18 questions, not exhaustive literature coverage or a PRISMA claim.

## Search by question

Three branches: official competence frameworks; intervention design/outcome studies; family/facilitator experience. Combine AI literacy, children, parents, K-12, generative AI tutoring, independent assessment, transfer, retention and randomized trial with topic/age. Vietnam/Vietnamese need a separate expansion; international samples do not substitute. Log date, query, URL, include/exclude/pending and reason; search rank is not quality.

## Verified intake

1. Confirm authors/publisher/version/date; read the original rather than retellings.
2. Save raw snapshot/SHA and verify actual content. HTTP 200 is insufficient.
3. State capture level: overview, abstract, full text or supplement. Extract only seen material.
4. Capture short exact spans, retaining document type; tier A/B describes source provenance, not effectiveness.
5. Compare DOI/authors/dataset/intervention for dependence. Blog/landing/PDF of one study are not three studies.
6. Retain disagreement/unknowns; run verifier/Refinery/SOT before generated publication.

## Experimental-study appraisal

Record population/age/country/language, recruitment, assignment, intervention, human support, comparator, duration, assessments with/without AI, missingness/attrition, funding/product involvement, model version, findings and limits. Unread fields remain null/PENDING_FULL_TEXT, not abstract inference. An RCT informs a causal question in its design; it does not establish suitability for another age group.

Frameworks inform goals, not intervention effectiveness. Qualitative studies inform mechanisms/questions, not demand percentages. Retain preprint/working-paper labels rather than removing them to appear stronger.

## Application decision

Compare contextual fit, resources, transparency and measurability. P02 mappings are AI_PROPOSAL for educator review. No “best method” claim or projection of international effect sizes to Vietnamese children.

Retain previous study versions, record new source/version, inspect diff/provenance and regenerate the library. Update [gaps](evidence-gap-register.md) even if searches yield many sources.
