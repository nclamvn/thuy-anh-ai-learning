# Communication channels · proposed structure

Status: AI draft · awaiting responsible review. Prepared: 3 October 2026.

Local design only: no external groups, actual members or sent messages. Choose a tool after understanding family habits and the operator's capacity to moderate. Discord, Zalo and all other services remain unapproved options.

| Proposed space | Invitees after confirmation | Content | Owner to confirm |
|---|---|---|---|
| Internal project | Lâm/Thuý Anh within agreed scope | Decisions, versions, issues, schedules, tasks | Project owner |
| Educational review | Invited educators | Lessons, rubric, unclear notes | Review coordinator |
| Parent support | Voluntary parents | Goals, operation, questions | Operator |
| Learner questions | Learners under agreed conditions | Task/checking/support questions | Moderating adult |
| Official announcements | Appropriate recipients | Confirmed schedules, fixed versions, preparation | Authorised publisher |

Learner-question channels are not open. Proposed adult moderation must handle messages; AI does not independently converse with children or decide incident responses. Private intake, response times, access and age limits need responsible decisions.

## Draft content rules

Use session codes for issues. Do not share learner images/work beyond permission. Separate sources, observations and hypotheses; label unconfirmed schedules proposed. Never promise infallible AI or unmeasured benefits, and do not publicly rank individual scores.
