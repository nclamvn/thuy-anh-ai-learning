# Measure what the AI claims

Status: AI draft · awaiting responsible review. Prepared: 3 October 2026.

**LES-01 · ages 13–15 (proposed group) · 45 minutes (design estimate, not measured).**

Distinguish area from perimeter, and identify and correct an incorrect conclusion through a calculation you can explain.

## Separate materials before distribution

**This is a facilitator pack; do not distribute it in full to learners.** Notes and rubric contain answers. Use the [learner handout](handout-01.md) for steps 1–6, releasing each part at its intended step. Show the sample AI response only at step 2 and source cards only at step 4. Collect/close previous work and sources before distributing the [separate independent task](transfer-01.md). Never print answers/rubric on its reverse.

## Comparison cards · open only at the comparison step

### Team-authored mathematics card · rectangles

The area of a rectangle is length multiplied by width; its unit is a square unit. The perimeter is the sum of all four sides: 2 × (length + width); its unit is a unit of length. This is a teaching card written by the team, not a research quotation.

## Deliberately flawed sample response

The garden area is 8 × 5 = 40 m². Since the fence goes around the garden, its length is also 40 m. Just multiply length by width for both questions.

**Team-authored fixture data**, not a response measured from a live model. Open only at the intended step; do not read facilitator answers to the learner.

## Draft rubric · facilitator only

The facilitator reads the checking evidence, explanation and independent task; scores manually against the rubric and records assistance. Do not infer general ability from one task.

### Verification

0: no check, or keeps the incorrect calculation. 1: recognises a need to check but does not perform a check. 2: partly correct check, with missing sides or units. 3: uses all four sides/the correct formula to correct perimeter and retain the correct area.

Score 0 / 1 / 2 / 3 / unassessed: ____ · Evidence: ____ · Assistance: ____

### Explanation

0: repeats the incorrect result or gives no explanation. 1: gives correct results without clear reasoning. 2: distinguishes the quantities but omits calculations or units. 3: explains area/perimeter, calculations and units in their own words.

Score 0 / 1 / 2 / 3 / unassessed: ____ · Evidence: ____ · Assistance: ____

### Independent performance

0: no independent work, or incorrect calculations for both quantities. 1: partially appropriate method. 2: one quantity correct, or correct results without reasoning/units. 3: independently finds 36 cm² and 26 cm, with reasoning and units; record any assistance separately.

Score 0 / 1 / 2 / 3 / unassessed: ____ · Evidence: ____ · Assistance: ____

## Why there is a transfer task

9 × 4 changes the numbers while retaining the structure. Work without AI provides one observation of near transfer; it does not demonstrate lasting retention or programme effectiveness.

Keep source cards, fixtures, facilitator answers and previous work out of independent-task view. If clarifying instructions is necessary, record the exact help and mark the changed conditions.
