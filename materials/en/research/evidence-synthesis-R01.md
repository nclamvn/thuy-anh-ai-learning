# Evidence comparison · R01

3 October 2026. AI synthesis of read sources, without educational review. Conditions analysis, not meta-analysis. See [cards](evidence-cards-R01.md) and [index](source-index.md).

## What can and cannot be inferred

SRC-03A secondary mathematics warns about performance after removing AI and the role of safeguards. SRC-10 tests a pedagogically designed college AI tutor and reports positive results versus active learning. These do not automatically contradict: learners, assistance, comparator and measurement differ.

SRC-12 UK secondary LearnLM has expert tutors supervising and able to edit every response. This is AI plus people, not evidence for removing teachers. SRC-13 reports a positive six-week Nigerian programme; its landing abstract cannot separate AI effects from added study time, teachers and the intervention package.

SRC-11 qualitative family co-learning informs interviews/roles, not the percentage of Vietnamese parents needing a product. SRC-08 2026 OECD/EC updates the 2025 draft and needs legitimate local capture before canonical claims. SRC-01/02 and existing guidance inform goals/responsibilities, not P02 curriculum effectiveness.

## Proposed design implications

- Keep initial, assisted and new no-AI work distinct; add delayed measurement after design/conditions are agreed.
- Describe assistance, intervention rights and teacher labour; do not benchmark only answer correctness.
- Choose one competence goal before expanding lessons; tool use and AI understanding need suitable observable tasks.
- Rehearse with adults to revise wording/rubric and usability; this does not establish effects on children.
- Effectiveness comparison needs matched goal/time and recorded missingness, designed by an educator.

These are AI_PROPOSAL, not changed child-use criteria or a winning-model prediction. Captured effects establish what authors report; full-text appraisal and replication remain subsequent work.
