# Data plan and falsifiable hypotheses · R01

3 October 2026. Proposed operations; no recruitment, response dataset or data-use permission obtained. Use [human handoff](../guides/human-handoff.md), [interviews](../templates/interview-guide.md) and [evidence](../templates/evidence-capture.md).

## Streams to return

1. Survey: exact question version, recruitment route, collection dates, invitation count if known, valid/missing responses and exclusions. Submitting a personal survey does not grant AI access to responses. No dataset is in this repository.
2. Interviews: a concrete experience, actual behaviour/support/difficulties/alternatives/counter-evidence. Use only permitted notes; distinguish reports from direct observation.
3. Adult rehearsal: fictional codes, lesson/version/device/step order, ambiguous phrases, assistance/errors and actual preparation/delivery/synthesis times. Not a sample of children.
4. Educational review: reviewer/scope/version, reasoned comments, revisions and bounded judgement. Local software does not authenticate identity.
5. Real trial: only after responsible conditions are confirmed. Agree assessment, no-AI tasks/follow-up/data scope first. R01 has not conducted it.

## Proposed synthesis schema

record_id, data_kind (REPORTED/OBSERVED/AI_DRAFT), rq_ids, source_ref, collected_at, recorder_role, task_version, context, observation, interpretation, counter_evidence, assistance, missing_reason, next_decision. Use codes instead of unnecessary identifiers. Missing data are null/UNKNOWN, not zero. Any future private raw records need separately chosen storage/access, never automatic inclusion in the local library.

## Hypotheses that may be rejected

H1: families prioritise verification over fast answers. Revise if actual experiences/choices show another priority; leading questions do not confirm it.

H2: the eight-step flow is easy enough to operate. Revise if adults repeatedly need clarification or skip steps. Do not invent a threshold before knowing design/resources.

H3: learners can independently perform a new task after support. Untested; needs suitable assessment and recorded conditions. A polished revision does not confirm H3.

H4: AI adds value over simple materials/prompts. Untested; needs equal-goal/time comparison and operator labour. A fast fixture is not evidence.

## Round output

Synthesis of answered/unanswered RQs, exceptions/missingness/fit and owner decision. Agree continue/revise/stop and responsibility before another round. No fixed sample size, minimum effect or price without design/data. R01 prepares discovery; it does not fabricate human answers.
