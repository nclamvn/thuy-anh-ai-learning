## Scoped checklist

Record each item's pending/in-progress/ready-for-adult-rehearsal/needs-revision status, responsible role, note,version/date,evidence. Interpret statuses locally; do not convert consent/review to approved merely to turn indicators green. Machine checks do not complete another person's interview/review/role acceptance.

## Structured observation

Code/session/lesson/version; synthetic/adult-rehearsal data kind; step/time; observed behaviour/quotation; reported account; interpretation; assistance; duration; issue/evidence; recorder role; missing reason. Use relevant fields, leave unknowns null/blank, distinguish reports from direct observation and omit identifiers in free text.
