# Project brief · what is known and what needs a decision

Status: AI draft · awaiting review by the responsible person. Prepared: 3 October 2026.

This local kit helps facilitators prepare learning materials, rehearse delivery and examine evidence in completed work. The project owner decides scope; Thuý Anh initiated the side project. Collaboration scope and decision rights between the parties have not been agreed in this document.

## First proposed trial

One practical session: the learner writes first, reads a deliberately flawed AI response, checks sources, revises and explains, then completes a different task without AI. The three [curriculum](curriculum/README.md) lessons are newly authored drafts, not an approved programme. Ages 13–15 and 45 minutes are rehearsal design choices; actual difficulty is unverified.

## Built locally

Local platform, draft lessons, observation sheets, research templates, draft communication channels and offline benchmark. Seed data are fictional. There are no child accounts, authenticated reviewers or real family datasets, and no service has been released.

## Proposed decision criteria

Can another facilitator run the whole session? Can the learner explain what they checked and complete an independent task? Record assistance to distinguish an adult doing the work from the learner's own performance. These criteria are not an effectiveness promise or approved pass threshold.

Use the [human handoff](guides/human-handoff.md), [decision record](templates/decision-record.md) and [evidence capture](templates/evidence-capture.md) to keep responsibilities, observations and interpretation separate.
