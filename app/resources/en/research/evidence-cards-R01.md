# Additional source cards · R01

3 October 2026. Four valid new captures, two queued. Selective intake, not full appraisal. Tier describes source type, not study quality. Canonical manifest holds raw files/SHA.

## SRC-10 · Kestin et al.

[Original](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-97652-6), Scientific Reports, 3 June 2025, DOI 10.1038/s41598-025-97652-6. Tier A, peer-reviewed research. HTML body captured; abstract/descriptive sections selectively read.

Randomised trial with college students compares a pedagogically designed AI tutor with active learning; authors report learning benefits. Not Vietnamese families or primary-age children. Read measurement/sample/timing/limitations before using effect sizes. RQ05/13: inspect teaching structure/comparison, not a claim that AI replaces teachers.

## SRC-11 · Xie et al.

[Original](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24070), arXiv v1, 28 October 2025, tier B preprint. Abstract/metadata captured; full text/peer-reviewed publication not yet appraised.

Focus groups with 13 parent-child pairs in middle childhood. Useful for exploring autonomy, co-learning and adult AI understanding; not representative demand estimates or product-effectiveness testing. RQ01/09/17: interview actual situations without generalising to every family.

## SRC-12 · Google/Eedi LearnLM

[Original](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.23633), arXiv v1, 29 December 2025, tier B exploratory RCT preprint. Abstract/metadata capture.

Authors describe 165 students at five UK secondary schools learning mathematics via chat. Expert tutors supervise and can edit every response. Reported results apply to those conditions. Developer/platform involvement requires reading methods/conflicts/attrition/actual interventions. RQ06/09/14, not autonomous-chatbot safety evidence.

## SRC-13 · World Bank working paper 11125

[Original](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/entities/publication/15e1ff08-15ae-4f7a-b2a8-d146e6c113ee), DOI 10.1596/1813-9450-11125, 2025. Tier A original institutional source; working paper, not a peer-reviewed journal label. Landing abstract captured, PDF appraisal pending.

Six-week secondary-school English programme in Nigeria using Copilot/GPT-4. Abstract reports 0.31 standard-deviation improvement in combined assessment. This is not a P02 target. Read paper/version/comparator/missing data/support/cost conversion; do not market “two years in six weeks” for Vietnam. RQ13/14/17.
