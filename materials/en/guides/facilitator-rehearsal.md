# A 45-minute rehearsal · facilitator

Status: local operating guide. Prepared: 3 October 2026.

Adults role-play using fictional codes to discover instructional and operational difficulties. This is not a study of children's learning effectiveness. Choose one [lesson](../curriculum/README.md), a facilitator, a learner-role adult and an observer.

| Planned minutes | Activity | Preserve |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Purpose, fictional-data confirmation, lesson version | No proven-benefit promises |
| 5–10 | Initial answer | Learner-role adult works independently |
| 10–15 | Read fixture and choose a claim | Do not reveal the error first |
| 15–23 | Compare cards and record evidence | Ask how to check; do not do the work |
| 23–30 | Revise and explain | Retain the learner's own wording |
| 30–37 | Independent task; close support | Record every intervention |
| 37–42 | Manual review and report | Leave unassessed if evidence is missing |
| 42–45 | Debrief, small change and owner | Feelings are not learning outcomes |

45 minutes is a plan. Record actual timings in the [observer sheet](../templates/observer-sheet.md). If the role-player needs longer, preserve evidence and stop rather than rushing to create an attractive report.

## Limited prompts

“What claim are you checking?” “Which source supports it?” “What does the source leave unknown?” “Without AI, how would you check?” Record the actual prompt. Do not rewrite the learner's work as an adult answer; “unknown” is acceptable.

## Stop conditions

If saving or sources fail, pause, preserve answer copies and log an issue. If the role-player still cannot understand instructions after clarification, retain the exact difficult phrase; do not change the rubric mid-session to manufacture a pass. For real children, people must agree participation/stop conditions and responsibility through the [human handoff](human-handoff.md).

Fictional example: DRY-01 uses LES-02; observer writes “could not find Notice B for 50 seconds”. This supports revising card presentation, not a conclusion that the learner lacks reading ability.
