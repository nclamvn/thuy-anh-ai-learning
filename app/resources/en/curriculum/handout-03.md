### Team-authored methods card · controlled comparison

To investigate the effect of a change, identify one thing to change and keep other relevant conditions as similar as possible. Choose a measure in advance, repeat trials to observe variation, and record anomalies. Without measurements there are no results; a few trials provide information only under the conditions tried.
