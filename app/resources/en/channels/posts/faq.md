# Draft FAQ · clear limitations

Status: AI draft · awaiting responsible review. Prepared: 3 October 2026.

**Proposed replies for operator review, not agreed policy or service commitments.**

## What is AI doing?

The local build uses team-authored responses with deliberate errors for checking practice, not a live model. Facilitators/reviewers enter comments manually; software does not automatically assess children's ability.

## Are answers supplied immediately?

The design requires independent initial work, comparison and explanation. The independent task closes AI, sources and history. Record any additional help; hidden support on-screen does not ensure no outside prompts.

## Have lessons been approved?

All three seed lessons are AI drafts. Local review records an entered opinion without authenticating identity or replacing educational approval. Educators/responsible people must decide real-use conditions.

## Is effectiveness demonstrated?

No. Research informs methods; these lessons have no measured real-group effectiveness. Satisfaction/completion alone do not demonstrate durable learning.

## Where are data stored?

In the browser and exported backups. Production permissions/synchronisation are absent. Agree data recorded, access, retention/deletion before real sessions. Never post private data in shared channels.

## What if a learner wants to stop?

Proposed response: pause, ask what they need and respect stopping. Agree actual conditions in advance; see [human handoff](../../guides/human-handoff.md).

Schedule, cost, recruitment, communication tools and contact: **unconfirmed**. DEMO examples are fictional, not participants.
