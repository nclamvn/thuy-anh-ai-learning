# LES-02 · design rationale map

Status: AI proposal · P03 draft; no accepted roles, educational sign-off or actual field results. 3 October 2026.

The anchor retains the existing LES-02 so started sessions do not change. This document explains design; it does not revise the lesson or confer educational approval. Narrow goal: apply an update rule to two fictional texts, retain unchanged information and identify what is unknown.

| Element | Observable evidence | Rationale/limit |
|---|---|---|
| Initial work before fixture | Information the learner plans to locate | Approach baseline, not actual club knowledge |
| Deliberately flawed fixture | Specific claim selected for checking | Checks content rather than confident tone; not representative model behaviour |
| A/B update rule | Text evidence for location/time/registration | Closed-text grounding, not web verification |
| Revision | Registration retained; cost unknown | Changes/retained/missing facts, no invented completion |
| Explanation | Why B overrides and parts of A remain | SRC-04 informs reflection; rubric is team-authored |
| Independent C/D | Room B,10:00,notebook,unknown registration | SRC-03A/05 inform assessment without AI; near transfer only |

SRC-01 informs human-centred goals; SRC-04 planning/monitoring/review; SRC-03A/05 separation of assisted performance from no-AI work. SRC-10/12/13 inform intervention/support/comparison conditions, not LES-02 effectiveness. [P03 appraisal](../research/full-methods-P03.md) states selective-reading limits. All mappings are AI_PROPOSAL.

## Possible design failures

Unclear update language; initial task encourages guesses before cards open; B is overlooked; rubric rewards copying rather than explanation; remembered answers substitute for transfer; facilitator prompts reveal too much. Review each against a task/version and evidence, not visual polish.

Reviewer first tries the task without answers, records reading prerequisites, difficult wording and whether C/D adds new skills.
