# Needs interview · avoid leading questions

Status: AI draft · awaiting review by the responsible person. Prepared: 3 October 2026.

Use for a 15–20-minute conversation after a real person agrees. Ask about a recent experience before presenting the idea. Do not promise an imminent product or treat praise as validated demand.

## Opening draft

“I would like to understand one occasion when your family used AI for learning. You may decline a question or stop. What may I note, and what may be included in a summary without identifying information?” The parties must agree the actual scope; this wording does not record consent.

## Questions

- Most recent use: what task, where, and how did an adult learn about it?
- What did the family observe in the work or explanation afterwards?
- Was it unclear whether an answer was correct? How was that handled?
- Where does the current approach cost time/effort? What tool or learning method was abandoned, and why?
- What would demonstrate independent understanding rather than access to an answer?
- After describing a trial session: what would not fit, and what else needs clarification?
