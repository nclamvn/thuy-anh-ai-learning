# Evidence capture · separate sources and interpretation

Status: AI draft · awaiting review by the responsible person. Prepared: 3 October 2026.

Every evidence row must reveal its origin and what it supports. Distinguish SOURCE_CAPTURED (what a source says), OBSERVED (recorded event), REPORTED (another person's account), AI_DRAFT (proposal) and UNKNOWN. AI_DRAFT is never field evidence.

## Record

- Evidence ID ____; date ____; original-record owner ____.
- Kind ____; original path/source ID ____; version/hash if available ____.
- What the source/observation actually supports ____.
- Permitted short quotation or specific fact ____.
- Context, session code and assistance ____.
- Synthesiser's interpretation ____.
- What this evidence does not establish ____.
- Contradictions, counterexamples and missing information ____.
- Use/sharing scope and location of non-identifying copy ____.
- Required action and owner ____.

Does not support: children independently identify misinformation, or programme effectiveness. Next: educator checks reading demand; facilitator rehearses.

Retain originals, list evidence IDs and exceptions in conclusions. Small rehearsal samples do not represent a market. The [source index](../research/source-index.md) records provenance; research links are not blanket approval for product decisions.
